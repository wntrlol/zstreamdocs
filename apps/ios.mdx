# Installing Z-Stream on iPhone & iPad

Z-Stream is installed by sideloading an IPA file. We recommend **SideStore** because it's free, easy to use, and only requires a computer for the initial setup.

## Recommended Method: SideStore

### Requirements

- iPhone or iPad running iOS/iPadOS 15 or later
- Windows, macOS, Linux, or Chromebook (only needed for the initial setup)
- Apple ID
- USB cable
- Internet connection

## Step 1: Install SideStore

Before installing Z-Stream, you'll need to set up SideStore.

Official installation guide:

[https://docs.sidestore.io/docs/installation/prerequisites](https://docs.sidestore.io/docs/installation/prerequisites)

If you prefer a video guide, here are some helpful tutorials:

- Windows: [https://www.youtube.com/results?search\_query=SideStore\+Windows\+Installation](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=SideStore+Windows+Installation)
- macOS: [https://www.youtube.com/results?search\_query=SideStore\+Mac\+Installation](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=SideStore+Mac+Installation)
- Linux: [https://www.youtube.com/results?search\_query=SideStore\+Linux\+Installation](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=SideStore+Linux+Installation)

Follow the SideStore setup guide until the SideStore app is installed on your iPhone or iPad.

## Step 2: Download Z-Stream

Download the latest Z-Stream IPA from the [Downloads page](https://zstream.mov/apps).

## Step 3: Install the IPA

1. Open SideStore.
2. Tap the **\+** button.
3. Select the downloaded Z-Stream IPA.
4. Wait for the installation to finish.
5. Open Z-Stream from your Home Screen.

---

## Refreshing Apps

Apps installed with a free Apple ID expire after 7 days.

To keep Z-Stream working:

1. Open SideStore.
2. Refresh your apps before they expire.

SideStore makes refreshing simple and doesn't require reconnecting to a computer after the initial setup.

---

# Troubleshooting

### Installation Failed

- Make sure SideStore is installed correctly.
- Verify your internet connection.
- Make sure the IPA downloaded completely.
- Try installing again.

### Unable to Verify App

- Ensure SideStore has been set up correctly.
- Make sure the required SideStore VPN is enabled when refreshing or installing apps.

### App Expired

Open SideStore and refresh the app before the 7-day signing period ends.
